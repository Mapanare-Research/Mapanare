Tensors in Mapanare are first-class typed arrays with compile-time shape verification. Create tensors with `Tensor<Float>[1.0, 2.0, 3.0]` syntax. Operations include element-wise arithmetic with NumPy-style broadcasting, multi-dimensional indexing, global reductions (sum, mean, max, min), and range-based slicing. The linear regression demo shows gradient descent using tensor primitives.
